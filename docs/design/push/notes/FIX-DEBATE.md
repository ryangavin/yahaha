## The fixes

1. **Lamp faces (app-wide kit).** New token `--lamp` lime #9fe04a (light twin #4f8a0e). Every on/off switch: ON = solid lime face, black label; OFF = the plain #161616 face (light-twin equivalent), #8c8c8c label, no bar. Applies to Accomp, Metronome, Unison, part On, Harm/Arp, Sound latch, L Hold, Looper, Store armed, Rack's In process, and every lamp button on Channel, Rack and Settings. Grammar: white block = chosen (tab, list row, loaded rack, primary action); lime block = on; 1px outline = waiting/queued/armed. The 2px bar survives only inside solid pads and on Start/Stop's green lamp. Idle Sound loses its 40% bar (plain off face).
2. **The count row (every board).** The section row (Stage-Dark ~:64-72, copied verbatim on every board) gets, in its empty middle, left to right: four 24×24 beat blocks (current = solid Main hue, beat one always has a white top edge, past = #333), "Bar 3/4" at 18px, "Main B" in the Main hue then "Main C" in the outlined waiting face, "fill after bar 4" in white. 18px type, fits the 32px row, ~570px. Identical on all 48 boards (token swap on light).
   - The Stage display drops its own beat dots and bar/progress row; keeps Main B at 44px solid and "Main C" at 36px outlined in the hue (not 28px grey); bar-progress line, if kept anywhere, is 6px.
   - Every compact now-playing block (tall pages and display pages) drops its beat, bar and next; keeps style, tempo, chord, playing section; gives the freed space to its content.
3. **Chord "held" face (Stage).** Add a small note on the Stage board (in canvas-note style, outside nothing; or a tiny second-state caption) only if it fits: the chord readout dims to muted grey with a small "held" when detection is unsure. Do not change the default playing face. Mark it "new" in a board note if the canvas has a notes mechanism; otherwise skip drawing, just leave the default.
4. **One Touch (Stage + every app bar/style line that shows "One Touch 2").** Four 32px buttons 1-4 where the label sits; the applied one (2) wears the white chosen face; clicking applies. The rack readout under it stays. Hardware: Shift + pads 9-12 = OTS 1-4 (show it where the boards document hardware/Shift, e.g. PadsPage2 or SettingsLaunchkey help text). Applying OTS never opens a dialog: switch now, keep "Recovered: <rack>", say so on the status line.
5. **Sound latch (PadsPage2, Stage).** One-shot: a click on Sound shows Racks until the next pad tap (screen or hardware), or until Sound is clicked again / fader button 6 is pressed. No timer. Latched: Sound is the lime face; the pad header's "Racks" is a lime block (no dashed box); the pager reads "Racks · Sound" instead of "Bank 1/5". While latched, the lit pad's caption reads "Save over".
6. **Rack pads (PadsPage2, QuickRacks).** Plain tap on the lit Quick Rack pad recalls the rack clean; if modified, the unsaved state is kept as "Recovered: Sunday drive". Hold Sound + tap the lit pad (or a long press / right-click on screen) saves over it: keeps "Previous: Sunday drive" in My racks, the status line shows "A1 saved over Sunday drive · Undo", the pad flashes white once. Update every help/status text on those boards that says "no confirm on overwrite" etc.
7. **Fader layers (Effects; the Stage tab look on every full band).** In a non-Vol layer strips 1-4 change face: part-hue meters and bracket go; bracket and fill go white; meters hidden; the value carries the layer word ("Rev 40"); Pan (if drawn) uses a centre-detent bracket; part names keep their hue. The fader header reads "Faders · Reverb" in white; the active layer tab is the white chosen block (replacing #3a3a3a). Launchkey fader buttons light in the layer hue (note it in Effects' help text). The layer persists across pages; no fallback timer.
8. **Half band (the 12 tall pages; canonical in Channel).** Pads fill their ~66px column (66×28, same radius). Captions are real, unique, 12px: Intro I-III, Sync start ▶, Ending I-III, Auto fill, Main A-D, Break, Tap, Sync stop ■, Start (match the actual pad order on the board). Queued = 1px outline + lit 2px bar in the hue with bloom + white caption. The Pads header reads "Sections · next Main C" with "next Main C" in the Main hue at 14px.
9. **Health slot (every app bar).** After the Launchkey dot: calm = "Audio" in muted grey, no number. The app really has a CPU meter (`cpu`, #340) and `dropouts`, so trouble states are: load above ~70% shows "CPU 74%" in Ending red; a dropout shows "2 dropouts" in red; 3 in 30 s reads "3 dropouts · buffer 256?". A failed plugin shows "R3 failed" in red; on its strip a red ✕ replaces the orange ⚠ (the orange ⚠ stays for "missing"). Draw the calm state on all boards; draw one trouble state on SettingsSystem (and keep its "Audio load" readout, it is real: label it "CPU").
